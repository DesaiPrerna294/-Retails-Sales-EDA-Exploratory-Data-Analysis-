# Retail Sales — Exploratory Data Analysis

Exploratory analysis of a retail sales dataset (4,310 rows, 21 columns) to uncover 
sales trends, customer patterns, and business opportunities.

## Highlights
- Cleaned real-world messiness: mixed casing, inconsistent dates, blank rows, outlier handling
- 9-part EDA covering trends, seasonality, customer segments, and product performance
- 4 data-backed business recommendations

## Tools
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook
